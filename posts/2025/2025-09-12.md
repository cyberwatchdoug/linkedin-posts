Setting up a multi-VM Kubernetes cluster? Don't skip these critical foundation elements.

🔢 Static IP Addresses
Prevent failures in service discovery and certificates. K8s relies on consistent node identity.

🔠 Unique Hostnames
Ensures accurate cluster membership and registrations. Essential for monitoring, logging, and troubleshooting.

⌚ NTP Synchronization
Critical for certificate validation, log correlation, and distributed system consensus

📳 Cross-Hypervisor Network Communication
Nodes must be able to talk with each other for cluster formation, inter-pod networking, and service mesh functionality. Some important ports to consider 6443 (API), 2379-2380 (etcd), and CNI-specific ports.

📣 Why this matters most:
In enterprise architecture, reliability is everything. Inconsistent networking leads to split-brain scenarios affecting cluster quorum; time drift causes cascading authentication failures; and improper firewall rules create security vulnerabilities risking entire applications and data.

👉 Bonus tip:
Document your networking topology and port matrix completely, and store it securely. Your future self and your team will thank you.
