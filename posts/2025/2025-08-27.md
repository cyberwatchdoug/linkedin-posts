If I'm being honest, my job hunt process is part mission control and part digital museum.

As a self-confessed job description hoarder, I save everything. Every intriguing role and requirements list. It all goes into my Obsidian vault title "Job Hunt".

From there, it's all about 🌀 organized chaos: frontmatter, tags, and a dedicated page utilizing the Dataview plugin to keep every opportunity on my radar. (If it's not tracked, did it even happen?) 🤔 

Even if a job posting lacks a requirement for a cover letter, I'm writing one anyways. It's good practice, even if it's wasted and never sent off. Every word in my resume is stress-tested with focused keywords and my cover letters don't just echo my experience, they mirror the demands of the role. I've even been known to make some rather punny ones! 🤡 

None of this can happen without GenAI thrown in the mix, this is 2025 after all. I mainly use it for quick spellchecks, grammar, and flow. But occasionally for suggested edits.

But the real magic is in the meticulous tracking and continuous iteration, which is true to my cybersecurity roots.

Sorry I don't have any stepped guide for you. Just a peak under the hood of how I treat my job search like a red team engagement: structured, relentless, and always with an eye for weak spots. (Where I'll switch to blue team and fix my process).

What does everyone else do for their job search and tracking applications?
