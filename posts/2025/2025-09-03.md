Anyone else feel like the cybersecurity job search is like tossing your resume into a black box and hoping for a spark?

I often see roles with lengthy requirements lists, but rarely do job descriptions make it clear what actually matters most.

Instead it's a guessing game, trying to reverse-engineer which skills or keywords will get past ATS algorithms, rather than winning over the humans who might actually read my story.

In reality, I like to apply for roles where I'm a 60-70% match, not just the ones where I already tick every box. Isn't the real growth found outside our comfort zones?

If I'm not challenged where I am, then I'm preparing myself for growth somewhere else. Yet it feels like the bar for mid to senior roles keeps rising higher and higher, especially for those (like me) aiming to take that next step into technical leadership.

I know I'm not alone feeling this way. The process is exhausting and sometimes demoralizing. But I'm committed to staying positive and learning from each experience.

Hoping that one day, there's more transparency and a real conversation writ large, so candidates and teams can thrive together.

For others on the job hunt, keep after it. Our spark will come.
