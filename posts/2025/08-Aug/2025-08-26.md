Are you relying on "likely legitimate" or "provably legitimate" for identity security?

Most organizations lean on probabilistic tools like UEBA to spot suspicious behavior, but attackers can outsmart the algorithms with stolen credentials, AI, and deepfakes.

That's why forward-thinking organizations blend deterministic methods into their workflows (like biometrics and cryptographic keys).

The result? An identity defense that sees broadly and verifies with certainty.

The threat landscape today requires a strategy of combining probabilistic methods with deterministic proof to go from "maybe safe" to "secure by design."

The hybrid approach provides both breadth and certainty. UEBA gives you the wide visibility across the enterprise while deterministic approaches eliminate impersonation risks which is where assurance matters most.

💡 In short: probabilistic for detection, deterministic for proof. Together, they form a foundation for modern identity resilience.
