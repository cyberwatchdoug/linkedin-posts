I spent time this weekend researching and planning a complete homelab refresh centered around Kubernetes orchestration.

My goal? Running all my self-hosted services and platforms with better automation and scalability.

My reason for Kubernetes? Two key drivers: skill development and long-term efficiency.

While the initial configuration overhead will be significant, the orchestration and automation benefits will pay dividends down the road. It's also an excellent way to strengthen my systems architecture capabilities.

My Research and Development Stack:
✴️ Perplexity AI for research - absolute game changer for quickly validating sources and diving deep into technical documentation
✴️ Ansible playbooks for configuration management and backups (natural fit for K8s deployment)
✴️ GitHub Copilot for code templating and surfacing improvements I might miss initially
✴️ VSCode as my development environment

The research phase with Perplexity helped me map out a solid strategy before jumping into implementation.

Having Copilot suggest optimizations and catch potential issues has been invaluable over the past month.

🎉 Now I have a strategic plan I'm genuinely excited about - Kubernetes at the core, wrapped in Ansible automation. This is just the beginning of what should be a fascinating technical journey.
