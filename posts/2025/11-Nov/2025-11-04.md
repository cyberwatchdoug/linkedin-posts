As someone preparing for the CISM exam and aiming for a role in IT management, I've been reflecting on what information security governance really means.

On the surface, governance might seem like just policies and paperwork. But for me, it's about leadership, accountability, and aligning security with the goals of the organization.

Reviewing my study materials for the CISM has challenged me to look beyond technical solutions, and instead get comfortable with building frameworks that anticipate threats, clarify roles, and ensure that security is part of the organization's core DNA.

It's not just something tacked on for compliance. Every policy, every risk assessment, every team decision matters.
