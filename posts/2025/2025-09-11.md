Learning Kubernetes? Start with Nginx. Here is why it's the perfect first deployment.

✅ Immediate feedback - simply curl and see it working
✅ Lightweight - Minimal resources, quick startup
✅ Simple - No complex dependencies, no databases
✅ Focus on K8s concepts - Not troubleshooting at the start

When you're learning container orchestration, like I have been, you want to spend time understanding deployments, services, and pods. Not debugging issues. I believe Nginx gives that clean learning experience.

For me, I got started with k3s and the Nginx:alpine container. It's lightweight and reduced resource usage makes it perfect for small homelab clusters!
