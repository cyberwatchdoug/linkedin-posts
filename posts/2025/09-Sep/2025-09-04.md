You often hear people say that in cybersecurity you should pick a lane and specialize. 

But what if GenAI just bulldozed the lanes and setup site-to-site VPN? 

Being a jack-of-all-trades used to mean you did a bit of everything. Now it means you automate everything, translate the technical to executives, and still have time for coffee or tea ☕ 

In 2025, versatility shouldn't just be survival, it should be the cybersecurity professional's superpower. 🚀🔐

Specialization has it's merits and role to play, just the same that a generalist must still command expertise under the hood so as not to blindly accept what GenAI spits out.

Maybe the old saying needs an upgrade: Jack of all trades, GenAI-powered master of some.

Who else feels like the real superpower is juggling everything, while still crushing those KPI's before lunch? 🦾
