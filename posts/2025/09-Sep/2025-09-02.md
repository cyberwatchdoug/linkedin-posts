The new LinkedIn algorithm magically shows you posts that somehow feel hyper-relevant to your interests, job, or the exact thing you were just thinking about!

That's good 'ol enrichment folks!

🔍LinkedIn's "most relevant" feed is just pulling context from your profile and activity to prioritize what shows up (see small text in the photo). It is attempting to curate what matters most to you: boosting signal, reducing noise.

Sounds familiar? Yep, that's exactly what we do in cybersecurity.

🛡️In threat detection and response, raw alerts are exceptionally noisy and often meaningless on their own. But enriching them with contextually data and through correlations and suddenly we can turn them into non-events or a high alert incident.

The algorithms and logic behind the LI feed and your SIEM aren't that different.
✅ data collection.
✅ contextual enrichment
✅ prioritization based on relevance and risk
✅ occasional false positives

Context is certainly king in both environments.
