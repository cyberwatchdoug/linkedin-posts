You don’t really understand something unless you can explain it to your grandmother.

The ability to simplify complex, technical topics for any audience is something I've been practicing for years.

It's a skill worth investing in for a number of reasons:

🚀 Influence & Leadership

Stakeholders, teammates, and executives are more likely to understand, support, and act on you input.

In short, clear communication builds trust.

🌐 Better Collaboration Across Teams

We don't all speak the same technical language, and cross-functional work is the norm.

Fewer misunderstanding means fewer mistakes.

🧠 Stronger Personal Understanding

Forces you to understand the topic deeply; a true test of mastery.

You can build better solutions because you truly "get it."

At the end of the day, being able to translate complex concepts into simple terms makes all the difference.
