Why I built and maintain my own homelab environments:

⛑️ Safe Experimentation 

I can test new tools, techniques, and workflows. 
I get to break things (not always on purpose) and fix them.
No risk to the business or enterprise devices.

🤝 Hands-on Learning

A controlled environment to practice and master skills.
Essential to gain practical experience.

🚀Career Advancement 

My homelab directly contributes to my professional growth.
Improving my design methodology and troubleshooting capability.

My homelab is an essential building block to reach my full potential and continuous improvement in myself.

What's more, it's simple and straightforward to get started. You don't even need a separate device!
